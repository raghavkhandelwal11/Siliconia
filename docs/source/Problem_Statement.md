# Problem Statement

## Problem Statement and Client requirements

### Basic Idea based on crude conversation with Client

Based on the discussion with the client, we understand that the basic idea is to build an ecommerce app to sell all kinds of  Electronics gadgets and tools to provide people with a variety and quality of the best  products available in the market. The platform also should have a proposed subscription plan.

The users who are subscribed get roughly 80% discount on newly launched local (Made In India) products exclusive to our platform based on first come first serve. This heavy discount is intended to make people aware of local products and promote the small startups with great vision.

Another core feature discussed of the app was to create an ecosystem where products can be seen with ratings from various Techies and youtuber influencers.

If any youtuber or promoter wants to review the product they must be able to sign up as a content creator profile and there should be a minimum criteria to be fulfilled.

Another feature that came up in conversation was to design a reward system. All subscribed members must get some reward points on every purchase which can be used later to add as a discount or as a whole purchase (if rewards points are enough to pay fully).

And we also discussed some other features like customer reviews and ratings after purchase, product feedback for manufacturers by customers, products of the week (best products based on sales and reviews), and recommended products based on previous purchases.

### Problem Statement

In the fast growing markets there is a big supply and demand gap for electronic products. Other established ecommerce platforms provide a lot of products and variety but this platform will try to integrate not just popular but also local products that are looking for a marketplace. The aim is to organically integrate social media influencers, promoters and  tech savvy people into the system to test and rate products so that the customers can have a better idea about it during purchase. Also the platform helps the entrepreneurs to get feedback from experts and customers. A reward point system and limited stock product launch discount (more than 80%) will be given.
